# Galaxy Tab S9 FE+ — Full Apps & Services Reference (with descriptions)
Use this to decide what to keep vs. uninstall/disable. **User apps** can be uninstalled normally (Settings > Apps or long-press > Uninstall). **System apps** generally can only be *disabled* (Settings > Apps > [app] > Disable), not uninstalled without root. **Services** below are not independently removable — each is explained so you can judge whether the *feature* it powers matters to you; if not, the way to turn it off is to disable/uninstall the app that owns it (noted where relevant).
